FAQ

Questions

Do you pull the permit under your license?

No. The permit stays under your contractor license or the owner-builder. We prepare and submit it as your authorized agent, track every comment, and keep you updated.

Are county permit fees included?

No. Our fee covers our work. Government permit and plan review fees are paid to the county or city and passed through at cost.

What do I get with a takeoff?

An Excel quantity sheet organized by item and area, plus a marked-up PDF so you can see exactly what was counted. You add your own pricing.

How do I pay?

After you approve the confirmed quote, we send a payment link. Takeoffs are paid upfront; permits are 50% to start and 50% at submittal.
